### About Me

I am a Computer and Automation Engineering Master's graduate from **Università Politecnica delle Marche (UNIVPM)** (graduated on **October 16, 2026** with **110/110 cum laude / with Honors**). 

I design and build end-to-end software and AI architectures across the full computing spectrum—from **HPC Supercomputing (NVIDIA A100 / SLURM)** and **3D/Satellite Computer Vision** to **Scalable Cloud Backends**, **Mobile Apps**, and **Real-Time Embedded Edge AI (STM32)**.

* **M.Sc. Thesis:** [*Traffic-Driven EV Queuing Predictor*](https://github.com/Sebastianelli-Nicola/Traffic-Driven-EV-Queuing-Predictor) — End-to-end Time Series Forecasting & Machine Learning pipeline (SVR, RF, LSTM, Prophet, NeuralProphet, SARIMA) accompanied by a custom [Automated Python EV Station Tracker](https://github.com/Sebastianelli-Nicola/automated-ev-station-tracker).
* **1st Place Winner @ Hack-AI-thon 2026:** Co-creator of [**FarmRoots**](https://github.com/Sebastianelli-Nicola/FarmRoots-EUDR-Deforestation-AI) (Team *ByteBandits*), awarded the **Premio "Maurizio Panti" 2026**, the **Loccioni Award** (*Completeness & Marketability*), and two **Confindustria Pesaro Urbino Awards** (*Best Business Idea* & *Application Innovativeness*). Featured on **[ANSA Marche](https://www.ansa.it/marche/notizie/universita_politecnica_delle_marche/2026/03/30/lia-contro-la-deforestazione-il-progetto-vince-il-premio-panti_68b4507b-2309-4c2c-be93-eb612bdc6206.html)**.
