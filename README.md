### About Me
I hold a Master's Degree in Computer and Automation Engineering from **Università Politecnica delle Marche (UNIVPM)**, graduated in October 2026 with **110/110 cum laude (with Honors)**. I specialize in designing end-to-end intelligent systems—ranging from **Deep Learning & Satellite Computer Vision** to **Edge AI** and **Scalable Backend Architectures**.

* **M.Sc. Thesis:** *Traffic-Driven EV Queuing Predictor* — End-to-end Time Series Forecasting & ML pipeline for urban EV charging infrastructure in Milan.
* **Core Focus:** Machine Learning, Computer Vision (U-Net, NeRF, 3DGS), Time Series Forecasting, and Backend Engineering (Node.js/TypeScript, FastAPI, Docker).
